# Video Ad Scripts — Investors Blueprint Webinar (Adriel Hsu)

2 short-form concepts (30-45 sec), filmable as-is on a phone. Both drive to the free webinar registration, not directly to the call.

---

## Concept 1 — "The Objection Killer"

**Hook (0-3s):** [On camera, direct to lens] "You don't need a real estate license to do this."

**Problem (3-12s):** "Most people think they need a ton of cash, a license, or years of experience before they can do their first real estate deal. That's why they never start."

**Agitate (12-20s):** "And meanwhile they're watching other people close deals with way less experience than them — because those people just had a system to follow."

**Solution (20-35s):** "I'm doing a free training breaking down the exact framework I use — from a 9-5 engineer to a full-time investor — called the Investors Blueprint. How to find deals, evaluate them, and structure them for real returns."

**CTA (35-40s):** "Link's below — it's free, but seats are limited. Go register now."

**On-screen text notes:** Overlay "No license needed" at 0-3s. Overlay "FREE TRAINING" + arrow during CTA.

---

## Concept 2 — "The Origin Story"

**Hook (0-3s):** [On camera] "I quit my engineering job with no backup plan."

**Problem (3-12s):** "I had a stable paycheck, a career path, everything that was supposed to make sense. But I was trading 40+ hours a week for a ceiling I could already see."

**Agitate (12-20s):** "Real estate had zero guarantees. No boss, no salary, nothing handed to me. Most people would call that reckless."

**Solution (20-35s):** "It's also how I became a full-time investor doing flips, wholesale deals, and multifamily across Texas and beyond — using a system I now call the Investors Blueprint. I'm teaching the whole thing free on a live training."

**CTA (35-40s):** "Link's below to save your seat — it's free."

**On-screen text notes:** Overlay "9-5 Engineer → Full-Time Investor" as a text card during the hook. Overlay "FREE TRAINING — LINK BELOW" during CTA.
