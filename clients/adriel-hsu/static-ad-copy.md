# Static Ad Copy — Investors Blueprint Webinar (Adriel Hsu)

4 angles, meant to feed cold traffic into the webinar (instead of straight to the call, which is the current setup).

---

## Concept 1 — Pain-driven (stuck/stalled investor)

**Hook/Primary text:** Still haven't done your first real estate deal? It's probably not because you're not smart enough — it's because nobody's shown you the actual system. Free training breaks down exactly how to find, evaluate, and close your first deal.

**Headline:** Stop Overthinking Your First Deal

**Description:** Free live training — The Investors Blueprint

**Angle note:** Targets the audience segment that's been "researching" real estate for a while without acting — speaks to the paralysis, not the dream.

---

## Concept 2 — Aspiration-driven (freedom/exit story)

**Hook/Primary text:** I quit my engineering job in 2021 to invest in real estate full-time. No trust fund, no inheritance — just a system I now teach. Free training on exactly how it works.

**Headline:** From 9-5 Engineer to Full-Time Investor

**Description:** The Investors Blueprint — Free Training

**Angle note:** Leans into Adriel's actual origin story — his strongest asset, since it's specific and real rather than generic "financial freedom" messaging.

---

## Concept 3 — Proof/results-driven

**Hook/Primary text:** Double-digit returns aren't magic — they're math. On this free training I walk through exactly how deals get structured for real returns, and the 3 mistakes that quietly wreck new investors' numbers.

**Headline:** The Math Behind Double-Digit Returns

**Description:** Free training — seats limited

**Angle note:** Plays to Adriel's "double digit returns" positioning; keep any specific number generic/illustrative unless a verified real figure is supplied.

---

## Concept 4 — Curiosity/pattern-interrupt

**Hook/Primary text:** You don't need a real estate license, a construction background, or $50k sitting in the bank to start. You need a system. Here's mine — free.

**Headline:** What You Actually Need To Start Investing

**Description:** The Investors Blueprint — Free Training

**Angle note:** Directly pre-empts the top objections (license, experience, capital) that likely make ad-to-call traffic hesitate before booking — this is the angle most likely to lift the cold-traffic response rate the ad-to-call setup is probably losing.
