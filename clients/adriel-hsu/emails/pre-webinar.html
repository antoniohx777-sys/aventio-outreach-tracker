From: Adriel Hsu <adriel@investorsblueprint.com>Day 1
Why I quit a stable engineering job for something with zero guarantees

Most people thought I was making a mistake.

I had a stable job, a steady paycheck, and a career path that made sense on paper. Real estate had none of that — no guaranteed income, no boss telling me what to do next, no safety net.

I left anyway in 2021. That decision is what eventually became the Investors Blueprint — not a theory, but the actual path I took and now teach.

More on the training soon.
— Adriel

From: Adriel Hsu <adriel@investorsblueprint.com>Day 2
The $17,000 mistake that taught me how real estate actually works

In 2018 I bought a 14-unit apartment complex. Within a couple years, a property management company I trusted failed me so badly it cost me over $17,000.

Then a tenant fell through the second floor. And I got sued.

I almost sold the building at a loss. I didn't — and what happened instead is exactly what I'm walking through on the training.

— Adriel

From: Adriel Hsu <adriel@investorsblueprint.com>Day 3
How a 14-unit apartment complex became an "autopilot" cash machine

That same building — the one that cost me $17K and a lawsuit — is now what I call an autopilot cash-flowing machine.

In 2022 I refinanced it. It appraised at $950,000. I walked away with roughly $252,000 back in my pocket, at a lower interest rate than I started with.

I'll break down exactly how on the training — not the highlight-reel version, the real one.

— Adriel

From: Adriel Hsu <adriel@investorsblueprint.com>Day 4
"I don't have $50K sitting around" (and other things people tell me)

The #1 thing that stops people from getting into real estate isn't lack of deals — it's the belief that you need a lot of cash sitting around to start.

That's not true, and it's one of the biggest misconceptions I address directly on the training — how deals actually get funded, and why "I don't have the money" is usually a deal-structure problem, not a capital problem.

— Adriel

From: Adriel Hsu <adriel@investorsblueprint.com>Day 5
You're not doing this alone

One thing that changed everything for me was stepping into the real estate investing community — forums, other investors, people who'd already made the mistakes I was about to make.

That's part of why I teach this now. You don't need to learn every lesson the expensive way, like I did with that $17K loss.

See you on the training.
