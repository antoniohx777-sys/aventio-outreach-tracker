# Researching a prospect

Goal: enough real signal to write a page that reads as "we clearly looked at your account," not a generic mail-merge. You don't need an exhaustive profile — you need one or two true, specific observations plus a defensible read on their offer and funnel gap.

## What to try, in order

1. **Fetch the Instagram profile URL directly.** Expect this to be partially blocked or to return a login wall rather than full content — Instagram aggressively restricts unauthenticated access. Take whatever you get (bio text, name, sometimes a post count/follower count) and move on. Don't retry repeatedly against blocks; one clean attempt is enough.
2. **Web search for the handle and the person's name.** Look for: press or podcast mentions, a linked website or Linktree, YouTube/TikTok cross-posts of the same content (often less locked down than Instagram and reveal more about tone and offer), reviews or testimonials, any existing landing page or checkout page for their offer.
3. **If the user supplied specifics in the prompt** (exact price, program name, niche), treat those as ground truth — don't contradict them with weaker inferred data.
4. **If you hit a wall**, say so plainly in the dossier rather than padding it out. "Couldn't verify follower count — IG blocked the fetch" is fine. A thin-but-honest dossier is easy for the user to fill in during the Step 2 checkpoint; a padded, half-invented one is not.

## What to capture

Keep these consistent with the outreach tracker's own fields (`index.html`) so the user can copy values straight across:

- **Niche** — what they teach/sell and to whom
- **Offer** — program name/format if visible (mentorship, cohort, course, 1:1)
- **Price signals** — anything suggesting price point (mentioned numbers, typical pricing for that niche/tier of following, "apply now" vs. self-checkout language)
- **Followers** — rough count, flag if unverified
- **Content themes/tone** — what they actually post about and how they sound (this feeds the personalization hook and keeps generated copy from sounding off-voice)
- **Funnel gap / bottleneck** — the actual reason Aventio is a fit: e.g. "bio just says 'DM me', no landing page," "running ads to a link-in-bio, not a dedicated page," "clearly doing webinars but with a generic slide deck," "no email sequence — replies go cold after one DM." This is the core of the pitch; be specific about what's missing, not vague ("could improve marketing").
- **Personalization hook** — one specific, true, recent observation to open the page/Loom with (a specific post, a specific claim, a specific launch) — generic compliments don't land here.

## What not to do

- Don't fabricate specific numbers (follower counts, revenue claims, testimonial quotes) you didn't actually see.
- Don't over-index on one old post if there's more recent content available — use the most current signal.
- Don't spend excessive tool calls chasing a fully-verified profile — this is a sales pitch input, not due diligence; a good-enough dossier that gets confirmed by the user in Step 2 beats a slow, over-researched one.
